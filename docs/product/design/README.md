# Design explorations

Visual concepts made with the owner before anything was built. Nothing here is approved
unless a line below says so; the app is the source of truth once a direction ships.

## Waiting for the coach

- Page: [coach-wait-concepts.html](coach-wait-concepts.html), open it straight in a
  browser. Also published at <https://claude.ai/artifact/NJemPT52znhtkLsrJDLxWZ>
  (private to the owner).
- Holds the tap-to-play games (P1 to P8), the watch-only sport switchers (W1, W2) with three
  caption sets, and the first calm round (A to G).
- **Owner's choice, 20 Oct 2026:** W1 (one athlete, a new sport every 4 s, a jump and a
  flip between them) with the "Working on it" captions, and **without** the sport name
  label. The captions describe coaching work the app cannot observe; the owner accepted
  that over the backlog item's no-invented-steps constraint. Later the same day: the wait
  is the whole screen, and a roadmap and a session fill get caption sets of their own
  (shipped in `src/components/coach-wait/`).
